On August 5th, 2026, the streaming platform Hulu put out a new show *The Shards*.

While I found the show to be an excuse to get cheap and attractive millennial actor slop produced fast and lacking the depth it needed, I was entranced by the pompous plot, and even more profound, the depth of narration of the show after diving three episodes in. At its core, the book is comprised of five individuals attending an elite prep school with a serial killer on a homicidal spree in the fall of 1981, Bret's senior year at Buckley High. His circle consists of Thom Wright, football quarterback, Susan Reynolds, lead cheerleader and student body president, and Debbie Schaffer, wealthy "it girl" and Bret's publicized girlfriend with father Terry, filmmaker a closeted homosexual with seductive tendencies towards Buckley students, and mother Liz, drug addict and alcoholic.

Starting that same fall, seductive playboy Robert Mallory joins the school, instantly earning Bret's distrust and jealousy. The book itself, as well as the show, opens with Bret on May 24th, 1980, driving down a California highway with the sunshine toasting his skin and wind in his face, on the way to view The Shining[^1] at the Village Theatre in Westwood. In the theatre, during his viewing a figure described as 

>...A guy around my age so stunningly handsome that I thought at first he was a move star or a model I'd fantasized about from GQ magazine... His face was a series of cut angles, and he had a full head of blond unruly hair, short brushed back... [the description continues]... A wave of lost crested hard in my chest and I suddenly ached for him—the sensation was so immediate and so tidal that I was shocked—and adding this new presence to the anticipation of watching the movie I was finally about to begin forced me to slo down my breathing... (31)

This incident in 1980 continually comes back as an incepting plot point, a point of consistent nostalgia for Bret. Eventually, Robert Mallory is introduced to the main clique, becoming an instant hit among Buckley's A-List. Conflict arises when, time and time again, Robert Mallory denies ever seeing *The Shining* on May 24th, 1980.

Also commencing during the Fall of 1981, and Robert Mallory's arrival, are a series of murders focused on students from Buckley High. These students are often marked days in advance, with signs like drained fish tanks, missing pets, or ritualistic symbols appearing on room walls, before each victim is violently mutilated. And who does Bret suspect this scandal to originate but bon vivant and recently-discharged shady kid Robert Mallory.
