about

I've always been passionate about figuring out how technology can help us create better versions of ourselves. Whether this includes productivity tools, avoiding addictive pitfalls, or creating QOL improvements, I want to create tools (not limited to software) that help people live deeper, introspective and more present lives.

This philosophy has guided my career, from education to defense and wherever the future leads me. My current work as a textbook developer for Kontinua has inspired me to pursue life-long learning in support of education. At Redwire, I have been able to alter processes that affect the entire company for the better. On my own time, I have reorganized my own life to be more efficient and minimize distractions around me.
