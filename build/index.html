Hi, I'm Arjan.

By trade, I'm a software engineer. By necessity, I am an innovator and designer. By passion, I am a cinephile and art aesthete.

These distinct but meaningful aspects of my life have shaped me into who I am today. After 13 years at a Classical Educational School in Minnetonka, Minnesota, I committed to continuing my education at California Polytechnic State University in San Luis Obispo, CA. My central coast endeavors have left me passionate about education, automation, and technological ethics.

Presently, I am committed to graduating from Cal Poly with a B.S. in Software Engineering in only 3 years. Outside of the classroom, I am often at coffee shops, searching for new third spaces, playing billiards, or watching obscure films no one has heard of.
